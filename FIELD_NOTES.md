# Field notes

Observations from real use, recorded against the questions in Part 8 of the
specification. The prototype exists partly to answer them, and none of them is
to be answered by design speculation when using the device would answer it.

Nothing is recorded yet. The first prototype scope (specification Part 7) has
not been demonstrated.

## Questions to answer in the field

- Does showing the QR from the Flipper beat using a phone?
- Do subjects scan it reliably?
- Is the display sufficient?
- Are two QR pages understandable?
- Is left and right the right interaction?
- Is a short centre press for a new session intuitive?
- Is a long centre press for termination both safe and fast?
- Is confirmation necessary or merely irritating?
- Can it be operated without looking once familiar?
- Does it survive repeated pocketing?
- Is cabling acceptable? Would wireless materially improve things?
- What status information is actually useful, and what is noise?
- Does the photographer still reach for the dashboard?
- Would a dedicated device need more buttons or fewer?
- Is presenting for an NFC tap comfortable for both people, and do guests
  understand which part of the device to tap without being told?

## Accidental terminations

Every one that happens is recorded here with the date and what the device was
doing, because `FD5` is revisited only on this evidence.
